Know if you can spend it, before you spend it.

Grant agreements have caps, deadlines and restrictions that are easy to forget. GrantAnchor remembers every expense your team records and checks each new one against the rules, so a travel booking in September can't quietly break a limit you agreed to in January.

  • ●Answers in plain words: go, careful, or stop.
  • ●Warns you when a limit is getting close, not after it's broken.
  • ●Long-term memory powered by Hindsight, so history is never lost.
